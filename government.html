The Problem We Solve

Fielding Velocity Outpaces Operator Readiness

New mission command information systems reach operations centers faster than watch floors can grow operators who exploit them under pressure.

The Readiness Gap

A powerful common operational picture platform delivers its value only when the operator on shift — at 0300, during a no-notice activation — can drive it with confidence. The system can be fielded in weeks. The operator takes deliberate, structured training.

WaveSurgeAI closes the gap between system fielding and operator readiness: validated Programs of Instruction, hands-on training on the actual workstations, and instructors who have stood the watch themselves.

What We Do

Core Capabilities

Structured operator readiness for Joint Operations Centers, grounded in National Guard mission sets and domestic operations.

Maven Smart System (MSS) Operator Training
Structured operator readiness programs for the Maven Smart System in Joint Operations Center environments: validated Programs of Instruction, hands-on practical exercises on government-furnished workstations, and scenario-driven assessment tied to real mission profiles.
Mobile Training Team (MTT) Delivery
Training delivered where the mission lives — on-site at state and territory operations centers. Our mobile training team model brings certified instructors, complete courseware, and mission-tailored exercises to the watch floor.
Train-The-Trainer Programs
Every engagement is designed to leave organic capability behind: instructor development tracks that certify unit trainers to sustain operator readiness long after the MTT departs.
Domestic Operations & DSCA Focus
Curriculum and exercises grounded in National Guard mission sets — Defense Support of Civil Authorities, emergency management integration, and the Title 32 operating environment — not generic software familiarization.
