Why HR as a Service

HR Built For Small Teams

Most small businesses can't justify a full-time HR department, but they still carry the same paperwork, scheduling and record-keeping burden.

One Platform. One Monthly Plan.

HR as a Service gives you the tools and discipline of an HR department on a monthly plan: new hires onboard themselves, schedules fill faster, hours are tracked, and every signed document is kept on record.

You keep running your business. We set up the platform, tailor it to how your team actually works, and stay on call when you need help.

What's Included

Everything In One Place

Digital Onboarding
New hires e-sign their agreement, handbook, direct deposit, emergency contact and license forms in minutes, with W-4 and I-9 tracked step by step so nothing falls through the cracks.
Staffing & Crew Communication
Post shift openings by role, collect confirmations, and fill gaps fast, with optional text alerts for staff who choose to receive them.
Timesheets & Payroll Export
Shift timesheets, break-compliance tracking and tip pooling, with one-click payroll export ready for your processor.
Records & Compliance
Signed documents and handbook acknowledgments retained and easy to retrieve, plus a full export of your data whenever you want it.
